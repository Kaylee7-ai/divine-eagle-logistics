What We Offer

Our Comprehensive Logistics Services

From customs clearance to door-to-door delivery, we provide end-to-end solutions tailored to your needs.

Import Clearance

Expert customs house agency services ensuring smooth and compliant clearance of your imports at Tema and other ports.

Export Forwarding

Reliable international freight forwarding for your exports with competitive rates and on-time delivery.

Warehousing

Secure storage facilities with inventory management and value-added services for your cargo.

Transit & Transshipment

Efficient handling of transit cargo and transshipment operations across West Africa and beyond.

Consolidation

Cost-effective LCL consolidation services to optimize space and reduce shipping costs.

Transportation & Haulage

Reliable road transport and last-mile delivery across Ghana and neighboring countries.

Cold Chain Management

Temperature-controlled logistics for perishable and sensitive cargo including agro products.

Door-to-Door Delivery

Complete end-to-end solutions from origin pickup to final delivery at your premises.

Agro Products Supply

Sourcing and export of premium Ghanaian agro products: cashew nuts, tiger nuts, ginger, sesame seeds and more.
